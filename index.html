Questions worth asking.

Do I need a phone number?

No. You sign in by connecting a Web3 wallet, and we never ask for a number.

Do you have access to my private keys?

No. Signing happens inside your own wallet. We never see, store or request your keys or seed phrase.

Can IO Talk read my messages?

No. They're encrypted on your device and only decrypt on the recipient's. We can't hand over what we don't have.

Are group chats encrypted too?

Yes.

What data do you collect?

No phone number, no email unless you add one for recovery, and no contact list upload. Our security page sets out exactly what metadata exists.

What happens if I lose my wallet?

With a verified email you can recover your account and bind a new wallet. Without one, recovery isn't possible — that's the trade-off that comes with not collecting your data.

Which wallets are supported?

Any Web3 wallet.

Is IO Talk free?

Yes. Larger group tiers and premium features are paid. Encryption and privacy are not features you pay for.
