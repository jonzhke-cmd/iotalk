    A phone number is not a login. It's an identity document — tied to your name, your bank, your location history and every account you've ever verified. Hand it over and you're trusting a company you'll never meet to look after it forever.

    One tap to sign in

    Connect any Web3 wallet. Your wallet signs the request cryptographically — no password to create, no SMS code to wait for.

    We never see your keys

    Signing happens inside your wallet, not our app. Your private keys and seed phrase never touch our servers.

    We collect no personal data

    No phone number. No email required. No contact list upload. Nothing on file linking your conversations to your identity.

    Add a verified email later only if you want the ability to recover your account and bind a new wallet. It's used for that and nothing else — leave it blank and we hold nothing.

    Encrypted end to end. Every message, every time.

    It stays between you and them

    Encrypted on your device, decrypted on theirs. Nothing readable exists in between — not on our servers, not in transit, not to us.

    Media is encrypted like text

    No backdoor to hand over

    Questions worth asking.

    Do I need a phone number?

    No. You sign in by connecting a Web3 wallet, and we never ask for a number.

    Do you have access to my private keys?

    No. Signing happens inside your own wallet. We never see, store or request your keys or seed phrase.

    Can IO Talk read my messages?

    No. They're encrypted on your device and only decrypt on the recipient's. We can't hand over what we don't have.

    Are group chats encrypted too?

    Yes.

    What data do you collect?

    No phone number, no email unless you add one for recovery, and no contact list upload. Our security page sets out exactly what metadata exists.

    What happens if I lose my wallet?

    With a verified email you can recover your account and bind a new wallet. Without one, recovery isn't possible — that's the trade-off that comes with not collecting your data.

    Which wallets are supported?

    Any Web3 wallet.

    Is IO Talk free?

    Yes. Larger group tiers and premium features are paid. Encryption and privacy are not features you pay for.
